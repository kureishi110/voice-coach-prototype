The change

Juno cannot say what something means until it has asked the caller, and they have answered.

One rule, on the order of two turns. It is the only change I would make, and everything below is either how you build it or a limit on it. Fixing this also fixes the accountability problem above, because a caller cannot credit Juno with a conclusion they reached themselves.

What it takes to build the rule

Four things, and each one can be checked against transcripts you already have before anyone touches a prompt.

What changesWhy the rule needs itHow you check it
1 Split the coach turn. A turn asks, or it tells. It does not do both. Today most turns carry a question and an interpretation together, so the question arrives after the answer and cannot do anything. Label the coach turns in old transcripts and count how many contain both.
2 Hold the read until they answer. This is the rule itself. It costs one turn. Check the order of turns. You can count the breaks.
3 Let Juno break it when someone is stuck. After two turns with no movement it can offer a read, and it says the read is Juno's. Without this, a caller who is stuck just gets asked more questions. Marking the read is also what stops them crediting it to Juno later. Count how often it fires. If it fires on most calls the rule is wrong, and I would want to know that early.
4 Carry their sentence into the text thread. After the call Juno texts a summary. That summary should quote what the caller said, with Juno's own version kept separately. Mine opened "Great talking today, Tanya! We mapped out exactly what you're looking for", and then described conclusions I never reached. If the caller works something out on the call and Juno's paraphrase is what gets stored, the rule has bought nothing. The quoted line has to appear in the call transcript as words the caller actually used.

How I would know if it worked

The number

How much of the meaning in a call comes from the caller. That is the middle counter above, running on real calls, visible to the people already reading transcripts.

The hard part

"Meaning" needs a definition two people can agree on. I would write the rubric, have two people label fifty transcripts, and check they agree before trusting the number at all.

What would tell me I am wrong

How many turns before someone stops talking. If holding the read makes that worse, it is not worth doing. I hung up at 6:33, so you already have a baseline.

What it costs

About one extra turn each time. 148 of Juno's words in B against 156 in A. Near enough the same length.
